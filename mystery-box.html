Questions

Before you order.

What exactly is in the box?

Brand-new Amazon returns and shelf-pulls — tech, toys, home, kitchen, tools and practical everyday stuff like chargers and cables. Every box is different; the find counts are guaranteed.

Is it really worth more than I pay?

Yes — every box is packed to a retail value above its price. That's the one rule our packers can't break.

Can I return a box?

Mystery is the product — boxes aren't returnable. If anything arrives damaged, message us and we'll make it right.

Members get special pricing on every box, all year. Joining is free.
